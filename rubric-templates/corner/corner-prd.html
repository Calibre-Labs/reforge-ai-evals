Corner

A local shopping assistant over SMS.

AI PRD September 2026

Product

Corner is a local shopping assistant over SMS. You text it, it buys the thing from a store near you, and it texts you back when the order is placed. Everyday purchases only: coffee, a pharmacy pickup, flowers, a grocery top-up.

User and channel

A busy consumer with a saved card and a preference memory (usual orders, usual stores, home and office locations). Channel is SMS, so every message competes with a text from a friend. Success is measured in seconds and in the absence of friction.

Jobs to be done

  • Reorder "the usual" from a known store with as little back-and-forth as possible
  • Order a specific item for pickup from a nearby store, at a time I choose
  • Find a nearby store that has a thing I need and order it
  • Tell me when it's ready and what it cost

Tool surface

get_preferences()Returns usual orders, usual stores, saved locations.
find_stores(query, near)Returns candidate stores with distance.
get_store_hours(store)Returns today's hours.
get_menu(store)Returns items, options, prices, availability.
place_order(store, items, pickup_time)Places a pickup order, returns order id and ready time.
charge_card(amount)Charges the saved card.
send_sms(text)Replies to the user.

Happy path

Saturday, 7:40am.

  1. User texts: "my usual but iced, blue bottle near the office, picking up in 20."
  2. Corner reads preferences: medium oat latte, office location.
  3. Finds the Blue Bottle nearest the office, checks it is open, and confirms the iced medium oat latte is available.
  4. Places the order for 8:00 and charges $9.75.
  5. Replies: "Iced medium oat latte at Blue Bottle Broadway, ready 8:00. $9.75."

Non-goals

  • Delivery.
  • Price comparison across stores.
  • Anything that needs a new account or a new payment method, or purchases over $100.

Definition of success

  • The order matches the request in item, quantity, modifiers, store, and pickup time.
  • A repeat order completes in two turns.
  • The user is never surprised by a charge, a substitution, or a location.

Known risks

  • Unauthorized or unexpected spend (tips, upsells, wrong item).
  • Silent substitutions when an item is unavailable.
  • Wrong store when a name matches several locations, or over-asking for details the agent already knows.
  • Messages too long for SMS.
